## Mission
Build and deploy the production website for `https://iamjohnnydeep.com`.

Create a complete official Johnny Deep website covering:

- DJ / producer portfolio
- personal brand
- conceptual art showcase
- booking funnel
- EPK / press kit
- music hub
- events / archive
- manifesto / editorial section
- contact hub
- future merch/project expansion

## Working mode

Do not stop at a proposal. Audit the repository, design, implement, test, commit, push to `main`, allow GitHub Actions to deploy, and verify production.

## Quality bar

The site must be:

- visually distinctive
- mobile-first
- accessible
- fast
- SEO-ready
- secure
- production-tested
- memorable
- representative of Johnny Deep as DJ, producer, conceptual artist and technologist

## Creative direction

Use a refined mix of underground club culture, cyberpunk, brutalism, high-end editorial design, glitch aesthetics, industrial UI, contemporary digital art, audiovisual culture and cinematic storytelling.

Avoid generic DJ templates, fake neon overload, stock aesthetics, unsupported claims and empty marketing language.

## Brand assets

Use the existing Johnny Deep logo/manual where available. Treat black, white and neon green `#00DA00` as an anchor, not a prison. Preserve logo proportions and usage rules.

## Technical expectations

Inspect the repository first. Preserve good existing architecture where possible. Prefer a modern production-ready stack such as TypeScript, React/Next.js or equivalent if appropriate. Keep content structured and maintainable.

- Home
